**Proposed acceptance.**

- Freeze the observed terminal graph, tied terminals, modifications, specimen/configuration,
  netlist hash and schema version. Measure each pot's total resistance, both
  wiper-to-end resistances, rotation direction and taper against physical position.
  Repeat forward/reverse sweeps, including stops and nearby positions; identify
  wiper/contact resistance and endpoint residuals without imposing ideal mute or
  unity-gain assumptions.
- Solve volume and bass together with frequency-dependent coil impedances, all
  stationary pickup branches, measured cable and receiver impedance, following the
  [network contract](../../specs/TRANSDUCTION_NETWORK.md). Distinguish coil EMF,
  loaded-harp, Stage-jack and receiver reference planes; specify terminal polarity,
  each transfer ratio's numerator/denominator, and the input-impedance port.
- Acquire a combined volume/bass position matrix covering corners, interior and
  endpoint neighborhoods over the declared bandwidth and resistive/reactive loads.
  Reserve entire control/load sweeps and a re-established session for validation;
  freeze final test groups before fitting. Require gain, phase, input impedance,
  transient and multitone predictions for every accepted condition, preserving
  absolute voltage and reporting failures individually.
- Characterize stimulus source impedance, leads, probe capacitance/resistance,
  channel gain/phase, grounding, noise and repeatability. Model or calibrate out
  fixture loading with propagated uncertainty. Predeclare acceptance limits after
  the pilot, including absolute residual limits near nulls and a separate numerical
  error budget; verify solver convergence and endpoint behavior independently.
