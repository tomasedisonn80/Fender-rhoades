# A017 — Magnetic reciprocity and equilibrium

**Finding.** [Architecture](../../ARCHITECTURE.md), [measurements](../../MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md), and the [supplied brief](../../research/USER_RESEARCH.txt) correctly require reciprocal induction/force, independent static attraction, and avoiding duplicate magnetic contributions. The [numerical contract](../../NUMERICS.md) explicitly identifies the one-way polynomial pickup as synthetic; its mechanical energy excludes electrical transfer.

**Missing contract.** Freeze each magnetic asset's port/energy convention: $v=V_a-V_b$, $i$ entering terminal $a$, and $F$ acting along positive mechanical coordinate $q$. Record magnetic stiffness, loss, and preload already embedded in measured modes; establish assembled equilibrium once. For reversible co-energy

$$W'(q,i)=-U_0(q)+i\lambda_0(q)+\tfrac12L(q)i^2,$$

require $\lambda=\partial_iW'$, $F=\partial_qW'$, and $v=Ri+\dot\lambda$. Thus $F=-U_0'+i\lambda_0'+\tfrac12i^2L'$ and $E_{mag}=i\lambda-W'=U_0+\tfrac12Li^2$. The brief's $e_m=-\dot\lambda_0$ requires opposite source orientation to passive open-circuit $v$. Voltage-derived $\lambda_0$ leaves $U_0$, hence $F(q,0)$, undetermined; constrain static force independently. Separate loaded modal losses before reintroducing the same electrical damping.

**Exact reference acceptance.** Couple an unforced synthetic mass–spring–damper to this map and an isolated coil terminated by resistor $R_L$. Fix parameters, positive differential inductance, and stable assembled equilibrium. Enforce $v=-R_Li$ and calculate $E=\tfrac12m\dot q^2+U_{mech}+E_{mag}$. Check

$$\rho(t)=E(t)-E(0)+\int_0^t[(R+R_L)i^2+c\dot q^2]d\tau.$$

Require $\max_t|\rho(t)|\le10^{-8}E_p$ under timestep refinement, where $E_p>0$ is initial perturbation energy above equilibrium. This is a proposed numerical tolerance, not an observed result. Independently check $\partial_iF=\partial_q\lambda$ and recover static attraction at $i=0$. Repeat finite loads and isolated open circuit. High terminal impedance alone does not eliminate currents between parallel pickup branches. Bare mechanical potential $U_{mech}$ must exclude magnetic terms already represented explicitly.
