# A028 — Contact solver events and failure bounds

The [contact contract](../../specs/CONTACT_REFERENCE.md) distinguishes separated,
active, and force-clamped branches but leaves switching rules unresolved. Track
`delta=0` separately from `k+c*delta_dot=0` at positive compression; zero force
does not establish separation. Integrate each branch’s stated loss over its
actual interval.

For allowed `0<p<1`, the active-branch derivative
`dF/ddelta=p*delta^(p-1)*(k+c*delta_dot)` diverges at onset when the bracket is
positive. An onset Newton Jacobian cannot be assumed finite. Never evaluate a
fractional power at negative compression. Any regularization or narrower exponent
domain needs explicit justification and convergence evidence.

**Missing gate:** Freeze a synthetic `p=1/2` fixture with a stationary hammer
tangent to the resonator’s analytic free-motion turning point:
`delta=delta_dot=0` inside a step, with separation on both sides. Add signed
clearance perturbations producing a miss and a short compression/rebound.
Endpoint sign-change detection can miss the tangent root or both roots of the
short contact. Require correct event classification/order without chatter,
localized event times, nonnegative force, continuous resonator state, and
convergent impulse, rebound and energy residual at `h,h/2,h/4`, with predeclared
absolute gap/time and scaled residual limits.

Declare iteration/subdivision caps and minimum time progress. Exercise budget
exhaustion: restore the complete accepted-state/energy-ledger checkpoint before
a documented bounded retry, or return explicit failure. Never accept an
unconverged force or silently skip contact. Separate offline budgets from runtime
deadlines.

Relaunch exactly at, immediately before, and after clamp/separation events for
opposite tine velocities. Freeze tie-breaking, preserve `q,v`, and account actual
hammer launch work. The [current impulse contract](../../NUMERICS.md) explains
phase-dependent input energy but cannot define finite-hammer relaunch semantics.
