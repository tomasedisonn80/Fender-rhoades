# A020 — Complex pickup impedance and loading

**Finding.** The [numerical contract](../../NUMERICS.md) and [implementation](../../../src/research_engine.cpp) retain silent branches but reduce the harp to equal resistors and a resistive receiver. They establish neither frequency-dependent loading nor internal-current accuracy. The [component inventory](../../../profiles/mark-i-stage-73/components.json) correctly leaves coil, cable, and destination parameters unknown.

**Identification gap.** The [supplied research](../../research/USER_RESEARCH.txt) reports documentary isolated-pickup DC resistance of approximately 170–190 Ω. This constrains winding loss near DC; it identifies neither inductance, winding capacitance, frequency-dependent loss, nor receiver impedance. Require winding polarity/connectivity, temperature, complex coil impedance over a stated band and stimulus level, cable capacitance/length, receiver input impedance, and probe corrections with uncertainty. Record tine geometry and restraint to separate motional impedance from winding parameters. Terminal spectra alone may confound winding, cable, and receiver capacitances; measure disconnected configurations separately.

**Proposed analytic fixture.** Connect two parallel branches, each comprising EMF plus series $R+sL$, with terminal shunt $C_p$. Set sources $(E,0)$ and receiver admittance $1/R_L+s(C_c+C_{in})$. Synthetic values are $R=100$ Ω, $L=0.01$ H, $C_p=10^{-9}$ F, $R_L=1000$ Ω, $C_c=4\times10^{-9}$ F, and $C_{in}=10^{-9}$ F. KCL gives

$$\frac{V}{E}=\frac{1}{2+(R+sL)[R_L^{-1}+s(2C_p+C_c+C_{in})]}.$$

With all capacitances zero and $R_L\rightarrow\infty$, winding currents are $\pm E/[2(R+sL)]$ although receiver current vanishes. Retain these currents for reciprocal-force assessment. Compare independent nodal magnitude, phase, and current residuals against this expression. Subsequently follow the [measurement plan](../../MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md): calibrated small-signal isolated/in-network sweeps, two known loads, and a held-out cable/load combination.

**Next contract.** Specify per-branch source location, polarity, impedance topology, state and current conventions, and passivity checks. Extend the present [load schema](../../../schemas/measurement-session.schema.json) with separate cable/receiver model references and measured impedance assets; retain scalar R/C only as a declared approximation with a validity band.
