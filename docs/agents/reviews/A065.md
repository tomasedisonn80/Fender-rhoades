# A065 — Parameter identifiability and sensitivity

Proposed rules extending the [measurement plan](../../MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md),
[validation contract](../../VALIDATION.md), and
[ingestion contract](../../specs/DATA_INGESTION.md); physical identification remains pending.

1. **Fix modal normalization.** Store coordinate scale/sign, modal masses, units,
   and contact/pickup participation. Verify coordinate rescaling preserves physical
   motion, force, energy and voltage. Scale-equivalent parameterizations must not
   count as independently identified solutions.

2. **Separate observable subsystems.** Constrain mechanics with calibrated
   force–motion transfers and free decay, conversion with synchronized local
   motion/voltage, and electrical parameters with independent complex
   impedance/transfer measurements. Hammer speed alone is not force. Forbid
   unrestricted force/pickup-gain/load compensation. Mark unconstrained terms as
   nuisance parameters, externally fixed with provenance, or unidentifiable;
   propagate their uncertainty.

3. **Make sensitivities comparable.** Record parameter transforms and nonzero
   physical scales, residual units, covariance/whitening, objective weights and
   derivative step checks. Evaluate the dimensionless, uncertainty-whitened
   Jacobian; retain singular values, rank threshold and weak parameter
   combinations. Report correlations, multistart alternatives and profile-likelihood
   or condition/session-bootstrap intervals. Local rank and optimizer convergence
   cannot establish global uniqueness; missing calibration covariance cannot imply
   independence.

4. **Design discriminating observations.** Select excitation locations, strike
   groups, pickup poses and known loads that separate weak combinations. Remeasure
   mechanics after geometry changes and track magnetic effects already included
   in fitted modes. Randomize feasible acquisition blocks, repeat baselines and
   reserve a re-established session.

5. **Freeze acceptance before fitting.** Store source/model hashes,
   ancestry-preserving split policy, parameter provenance, bounds/priors,
   initialization/seed, algorithm/version, convergence, exclusions and residuals
   by condition. Predeclare uncertainty/conditioning criteria and required held-out
   prediction metrics; numerical thresholds await actual repeatability evidence.
   Use validation only for declared model selection; keep final test conditions
   locked. A failed required condition blocks its claim. Unresolved parameter
   ambiguity blocks component-value claims even when predictive acceptance passes.
