# A049 — Calibration uncertainty and acquisition validity

**Finding.** [A047](A047.md) identifies unresolved calibration references and
missing dates. The [schema](../../../schemas/measurement-session.schema.json)
and [validator](../../../tools/validate_data.py) require finite nonnegative
uncertainty and channel-unit agreement, but define neither standard/expanded
uncertainty nor coverage, covariance, timing/phase response, or validity at
acquisition. These are gaps against [measurement-plan §§2–3](../../MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md),
not implemented guarantees.

**Proposed minimal version-2 contract.** Use resolved calibration records with
fixed fields:

- **Identity and applicability:** `id`, immutable `record_sha256` and locator,
  `equipment_serials`, `channel_ids`, `configuration_sha256`, `method_id/version`,
  resolved `reference_standard_ids`, `calibrated_at`, `valid_from`, `valid_until`,
  and numeric signal, frequency, and temperature bounds. Reference-standard
  records must resolve their own traceability and uncertainties; certificate
  prose alone cannot satisfy these fields.
- **Correction and uncertainty:** a declared correction-model enum with named
  parameter values/units, `u_standard` in each parameter's unit, and a hashed
  uncertainty budget. Optional expanded intervals require `coverage_factor`,
  `coverage_probability`, and `interval_method`, including distribution/degrees
  of freedom when used. Do not infer 95% coverage from a factor of two or label
  coverage probability parameter-fit confidence. Store parameter ordering and
  covariance with product units, including shared reference/channel errors;
  omitted covariance means unknown, not independence.
- **Timing and phase:** `clock_id`, `reference_channel`, `delay_s`,
  `drift_s_per_s`, sign/reference conventions, their uncertainties/covariance,
  polarity, and a hashed frequency/gain/phase response table covering analogue
  anti-alias filtering. State whether delay is already removed from that table
  to prevent double correction. Unknown synchronization blocks phase-sensitive
  claims.

**Future acquisition gate.** Add acquisition start/end timestamps; resolve and
verify record hashes, equipment/configuration matches, validity throughout that
interval, and operating-domain inclusion. Check finite uncertainty values,
coverage consistency, covariance dimensions, symmetry, positive semidefiniteness,
and diagonals matching squared standard uncertainties. Changed calibration
invalidates dependent acceptance until re-evaluated; preserve raw observations.

**Verification.** Read-only source inspection; no tests or physical calibration
performed. Only this review changed. Next: implement this versioned contract with
targeted rejection fixtures before accepting measured data.
