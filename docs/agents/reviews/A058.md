# A058 — Shared-circuit multitone and transient identification

**Boundary.** The [engine](../../../src/research_engine.cpp) ends at a synthetic resistive harp. Following [A023](A023.md) and [A056](A056.md), future `CIR-001` work requires one identified revision, connected source impedance, shared rails, declared initialization, and a calibrated output boundary.

**Proposed matrix, unrun.** Choose period `T` in seconds from the characterized bandwidth; the bin indices below are stimulus choices, not specimen measurements.

| Experiment | Conditions and observations |
|---|---|
| Sparse multisine and each tone alone | Frequencies `{17,43,101}/T`; three pilot-selected voltage levels; phase vectors `(0,0,0)` and `(0,π/2,π)` radians at equal RMS. Capture complex excited-bin and empty-bin responses. |
| Probe with paired bursts | Reverse unequal-energy burst order; vary interburst gaps across observed recovery. Capture probe gain/phase, rail voltage, aggregate current, and post-burst bias. |
| Electrical cross-check | Cross both experiments with two measured source impedances and two known loads, retaining all connected pickups. Repeat after independently re-establishing the setup. |

**Interpretation.** Synchronize clocks; calibrate polarity, channel phase, delay, and acquisition filtering. Analyze settled integer-period windows coherently; assess transient recovery separately with preserved timing. Compare against a converged higher-rate reference after documented filtering/resampling and latency alignment. Independently refine timestep and solver tolerance. Persistent combination-frequency products consistent with tone-alone controls support intermodulation; rate-dependent folding implicates aliasing. Neither classification follows from a single spectrum. Report complex residuals and acquisition floors. History-dependent probe errors correlated with rail recovery suggest missing shared state, while clock drift or unconverged solves must first be excluded.
