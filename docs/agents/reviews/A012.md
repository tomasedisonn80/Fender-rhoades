# A012 — Damper and pedal contact contract

**Evidence.** [Architecture](../../ARCHITECTURE.md), [measurement plan](../../MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md), and [validation](../../VALIDATION.md) require persistent state, individual clearance, contact trajectories, and energy accounting. The [header](../../../include/rhodes/research_engine.hpp) exposes only `set_sustain(bool)`; [implementation](../../../src/research_engine.cpp) switches modal decay through `update_damping`. This is explicitly a viscous-loss surrogate, with no damper position, contact compression, or linkage state.

**Missing criterion.** The documents do not specify an observable acceptance gate that separates continuous contact from a decay curve fitted to release audio. Require a continuous pedal coordinate driving one shared linkage, per-key clearance and key-lift geometry, and a unilateral constitutive law with declared elastic storage and dissipative terms. At separation, normal force must vanish without attraction; finite-compliance engagement must preserve resonator and damper displacement/velocity. Recontact must follow the evolving gap, including partial lifts and strikes during ongoing release.

Half-pedal identification needs synchronized pedal/linkage travel, per-key damper/tine motion and contact timing, plus electrical output. Sweep travel in both directions at several rates and initial vibration amplitudes/phases; record hysteresis and uncertainty. Hold out complete trajectories and keys with different clearances. A continuously adjustable decay coefficient alone cannot establish this mechanism.

**Passivity gate.** With linkage fixed and no external excitation, total modal, damper and contact energy must not grow beyond a predeclared numerical tolerance. For moving pedal/key actuation, compare stored-energy change plus nonnegative dissipation against measured/computed external work; tine energy alone can legitimately increase. Report cumulative balance residuals, force signs and timestep convergence across onset, sustained contact, separation and repeated recontact.

**Next task.** Add a bounded PHY-002 damper specification defining gap signs, shared/per-key coordinates, energy terms, acquisition channels, and explicit tolerances before implementing the offline reference. Keep synthetic numerical evidence separate from specimen identification.

**Verification.** Read-only inspection; no tests run. Only this review file changed.
