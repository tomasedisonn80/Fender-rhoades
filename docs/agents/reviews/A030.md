# A030 — Energy balance and passivity acceptance

**Finding.** [NUMERICS](../../NUMERICS.md) bounds current energy to independent mechanical modes; [tests](../../../tests/cpp/research_engine_test.cpp) inspect passive decay and endpoint lossless conservation. These cannot establish coupled passivity. The [contact](../../specs/CONTACT_REFERENCE.md) and [transduction](../../specs/TRANSDUCTION_NETWORK.md) contracts require reciprocal work, clamped-contact loss, actuator work, and cumulative residuals. [Validation](../../VALIDATION.md) lacks one common reporting convention joining instantaneous, event, and accumulated balances across changing controls.

**Required ledger.** Between events, independently compute instantaneous power residual `p=dE/dt+P_loss-P_external`, interval residual `r_n=E_(n+1)-E_n+D_n-W_n`, and cumulative `R_N=sum(r_n)`. Report `max|R_n|` and `sum|r_n|` alongside terminal residual so cancellation cannot conceal excursions. Include separate event rows in cumulative sums, with impulse work `J*v^-+J^2/(2*m)` and action relaunch energy. Include signed pedal/linkage work and work from controls changing storage parameters; ideal resistance changes contribute no storage work. Separate viscous, branch-specific contact/clamp, and electrical losses. Numerical damping remains residual, never a physical-loss credit. Include mechanical, contact, magnetic, and circuit storage without double counting.

**Proposed gate.** For PHY-002, then each coupled extension, freeze synthetic launch/relaunch, pedal engagement/release, and applicable control trajectories, plus passive and conservative limits. At `h,h/2,h/4`, require maximum cumulative residual and accumulated absolute residual below predeclared `epsilon_abs+epsilon_rel*E_ref`, with nonzero physical `E_ref`; demonstrate refinement or a documented roundoff floor. Freeze a separate instantaneous power tolerance; small solver residuals cannot substitute for energy closure. Repeat under `q'=(2*q1,-3*q2)` with consistently transformed masses, stiffnesses, and port projections: physical trajectories, energy, work, and loss must agree within frozen tolerances. With external work zero, stored energy must not increase beyond tolerance.

**Evidence/handoff.** Source inspection only; no numerical experiment ran. Next: implement the shared ledger and fixture before claiming reference passivity.
