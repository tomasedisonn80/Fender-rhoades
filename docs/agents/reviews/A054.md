## Proposed checks — all unrun

- Measure `Z(f)=V(f)/I(f)` with calibrated gain, phase and current sensing. Retain real/imaginary values, frequency grid, temperature and RMS stimulus. Establish a small-signal amplitude plateau, then test levels bounded by observed operating voltages/currents. Record harmonics and amplitude dependence; a small-signal fit alone cannot establish operating-amplitude validity.
- Calibrate open/short/known-load standards at the fixture plane. Characterize lead inductance, fixture capacitance, probe input impedance and cable response. De-embed acquisition hardware while retaining the specified instrument cable and receiver load. Preserve raw/corrected data and calibration identifiers.
- Acquire isolated coils and the connected 73-pickup graph. Record isolation/restoration, polarity, pickup geometry and mechanically free/clamped conditions. Stationary pickup sources remain electrical branches; mechanical silence must not disconnect their impedances.
- Record actual volume/bass settings, endpoint/wiper behavior and receiving impedance. Validate frozen predictions against independent resistive/reactive loads and held-out control combinations using transfer magnitude/phase, transients and multitone observations.
- Propagate calibration-standard tolerance, channel mismatch, noise, drift, reconnection variability and de-embedding uncertainty. Report poorly conditioned frequency regions. DC resistance supports continuity and temperature context; it cannot identify inductance, winding capacitance, frequency-dependent loss or loaded response.
