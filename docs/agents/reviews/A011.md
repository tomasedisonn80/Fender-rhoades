## Required precision before implementation

Define a common positive contact normal, hammer position `x_h`, initial clearance `g0`, and compression `delta = x_h - b_h^T q - g0`. Then `delta_dot = v_h - b_h^T q_dot`; use exactly the same participation vector for resonator force `b_h F` and observed contact motion. The hammer receives `-F`, with `m_h > 0`. This fixes signs and work reciprocity without assuming modal forces sum like physical free-body forces.

Declare finite admissible `k > 0`, `c >= 0`, `p > 0`, parameter units, and supported ranges. Specify whether a zero trial force at positive compression changes contact mode, how grazing/zero-compression events are resolved, and how relaunch interacts with an existing contact. Recontact must follow evolving geometry; no resonator reset is permissible. Account separately for energy supplied or removed by action/relaunch.

For the proposed clamp, use stored contact energy `U = k delta^(p+1)/(p+1)` when compressed. My energy derivation for an otherwise conservative fixture gives `d(E_mech+U)/dt = -c delta^p delta_dot^2` on the unclipped branch. When the trial force is clipped to zero, it instead equals `k delta^p delta_dot <= 0`. Therefore the clamp can remain passive, but using the unclipped dissipation formula everywhere gives an incorrect energy ledger.

## Discriminating acceptance checks

Require zero force across a gap; equal/opposite contact impulses; an undamped two-free-mass fixture conserving momentum and energy; damped impacts exercising the clamp; and moving-tine strikes at opposite phases followed through separation and recontact. Include repeated launch and damper/pedal engagement without discontinuous resonator state. Supported resonators require support-force accounting rather than blanket momentum conservation.

Freeze absolute/relative limits for energy balance, force residual, impact timing, impulse, and rebound speed across `h`, `h/2`, `h/4`. These numeric limits and contact-event rules are the exact missing acceptance details. Calibrate MIDI-to-hammer launch speed separately from relative impact speed, reserving entire measured speed groups.
