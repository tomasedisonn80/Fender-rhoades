# A026 — Equilibrium and initialization audit

**Finding.** [Architecture](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) places modal coordinates at assembled rest, but no initialization contract balances gravity, mounting preload, damper compression and permanent-magnet attraction together. The [current kernel](../../NUMERICS.md) zeros uncoupled mechanics during successful preparation/reset; its resistive network stores no charge/current. Existing startup-silence assertions cover that synthetic boundary, not a coupled operating point.

**Required convention.** Solve equilibrium before shifting coordinates to `q=0`; retain equilibrium offsets in contact gaps and magnetic-map evaluation. Distinguish gravity orientation, support reactions, preload and zero-current magnetic force from dynamic increments. Record which stiffness/loss contributions measured modes contain. The [transduction contract](../../specs/TRANSDUCTION_NETWORK.md) separates static attraction from linkage derivatives: zero motional EMF does not imply zero magnetic force. Initialize capacitor charge, coil current/flux and circuit algebraic variables consistently with topology and constraints; zero current can coexist with nonzero permanent linkage. Distinguish equilibrium initialization from a deliberately energized startup transient.

**Proposed synthetic gate.** Freeze a stable mass–spring fixture with gravity, nonzero preload, compliant damper [contact](../../specs/CONTACT_REFERENCE.md), compatible magnetic co-energy, and a passive RLC load. Independently solve force balance, contact feasibility, KCL and KVL. Predeclare scaled absolute/relative residual limits and nonzero energy scales. From equilibrium, require bounded drift and negligible startup voltage without muting. Apply small signed mechanical/electrical perturbations; check return toward equilibrium and total stored-energy change plus dissipated energy minus external work under `h`, `h/2`, `h/4` refinement. Report contact transitions separately.

**Missing provenance and lifecycle.** Add an equilibrium-state manifest: configuration/data hashes, coordinate origin, gravity vector, contact active set, preload source, magnetic inclusion flags, electrical initial states, solver/version, tolerances and achieved residuals. Hard reset restores this validated state. Live reconfiguration preserves physical motion and admissible electrical storage, transforms coordinates, accounts for parameter-change work, and rejects incompatible migration atomically.

**Verification/handoff.** Read-only inspection (`rg`, `cat`, `sed`); no tests run. Next: specify this gate before coupled-reference implementation.
