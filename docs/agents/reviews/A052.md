## Proposed acquisition and acceptance gates

1. **Acquisition readiness:** Freeze the pilot key, specimen revision, and
   regulation. Photograph hammer-tip wear, deformation, and material uncertainty;
   measure contact location, normal direction, rest clearance, and escapement
   with explicitly defined endpoints. Record key travel, hammer release/free
   flight, rebound, local tine motion, and loaded voltage on synchronized channels.
   Calibrate spatial scale, timing offset/drift, and sensor loading; quantify
   tracking, differentiation/filtering, occlusion, and repeatability uncertainty.
   Missing calibration blocks the affected claim.

2. **MIDI mapping acceptance:** Record the actual controller or sensing system,
   velocity settings, timestamps, and key mapping. Pair its MIDI velocity labels
   with measured hammer speed immediately before contact, in m/s. Keep launch
   speed and relative contact speed `v_h - v_t` separate. Fit a specimen/key/action
   condition-dependent mapping with uncertainty; absent native MIDI sensing,
   leave that bridge uncalibrated. Neither MIDI labels nor impact speed alone
   establishes force; force identification needs additional constrained evidence.

3. **Prediction acceptance:** Use the repeatability pilot to predeclare strike
   groups, repetition counts, and error thresholds. Reserve whole speed groups,
   retrigger intervals/tine phases, and a re-established session. Compare hammer
   speed, contact timing, separation/recontact, rebound, and loaded onset per
   condition. Include incomplete action return and ongoing tine motion; preserve
   their state. Report failed conditions and uncertainty separately, without a
   universal MIDI-to-force curve.

**Next action:** Acquire one traceable native-action pilot after specimen access
is available; these proposed gates remain unrun.
