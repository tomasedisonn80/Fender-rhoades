# A014 — Coupled tine, tonebar, and support modes

**Finding:** [Architecture](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) correctly requires coupled mechanics, mounting compliance/loss/preload, modal participation, and separate mechanical versus electrical harmonics. The [kernel](../../../src/research_engine.cpp) currently supplies three synthetic frequency ratios, fixed strike/pickup weights, and independent decay. Independent modal coordinates can represent a coupled structure after reduction; these particular constants have no identified coupled-assembly reference. They therefore establish neither tonebar energy exchange nor inharmonic attack-mode accuracy.

**Missing validation contract:** Require a spatial force-to-motion transfer record before accepting a structural mode set. For each excitation and observation, preserve position in metres, direction, assembled boundary/preload condition, and the shared modal normalization. Record complex transfer functions or synchronized force/motion histories at tine, tonebar, and support locations, including uncertainty and bandwidth. The [measurement plan](../../MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md) requests labelled locations and synchronized motion, but the [session schema](../../../schemas/measurement-session.schema.json) lacks structured excitation/sensor coordinates. A mode set should predict held-out excitation/observation pairs, including weak responses near nodes; matching pickup voltage alone cannot satisfy this contract. This extends the [validation](../../VALIDATION.md) requirements into a specific structural acceptance gate.

**Next reference experiment:** Build a labelled synthetic three-coordinate tine/tonebar/support mass–spring–damper assembly with reciprocal coupling and positive energy. Apply recorded finite-duration forces at two coordinates; observe all three. Compare direct-coordinate and reduced-modal predictions for transient phase, decay, energy transfer, and spatial response, with timestep/order refinement. Retain an inharmonic transient mode and hold out one forcing/observation pair. Then pass only reconstructed pickup-local motion through the existing nonlinear linkage law. Require generated voltage harmonics/intermodulation to remain distinguishable from the known structural poles; never add an oscillator solely to remove an electrical spectral residual. Later repeat the spatial protocol on the identified specimen.

**Verification:** Read-only `rg`, `cat`, and `sed` inspection; no numerical or physical experiment run. Only this report changed.
