## Proposed evaluator contract

Record action trigger, native hammer launch, geometric contact, force onset/clamp,
separation, damper engagement, and voltage onset/peak as separate landmarks.
Report trigger-to-contact, contact-to-voltage, release-to-engagement, and decay
timing errors alongside calibrated voltage and mechanical trajectory residuals.
Define voltage detector bandwidth, threshold, hysteresis, and windows beforehand;
an electrical threshold crossing must not substitute for mechanical contact.

For retriggers, match the existing modal displacement/velocity and phase,
hammer position/velocity/contact branch, key/action state, and pedal trajectory.
Retain pre-event history and distinguish launch velocity from relative impact
velocity. A retrigger during sounding motion need not create a fresh voltage
onset; compare its event-relative residual and state evolution without resetting
the oscillator or normalizing away the attack.

Use the synchronized physical timeline with independently calibrated clock/filter
corrections and uncertainty. Prohibit fitted shifts or time warps. Report event
brackets, missing/extra events, grazing ambiguity, and noise-floor limitations;
mark events outside acquisition windows as censored, never zero-delay successes.

**Future fixtures:** rest attack; equal launches at opposite tine phases; relaunch
before/at/after separation or clamping; held/released keys across pedal engagement,
partial travel, and reversal; coincident release/pedal events; grazing misses,
short contacts, and truncated records. Refine timestep, solve tolerance, and event
localization independently. Freeze per-condition limits from uncertainty and
repeatability before acceptance.
