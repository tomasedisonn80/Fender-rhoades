# A061 — Listening-study and statistics review

**Finding.** The [validation contract](../../VALIDATION.md#listening-evaluation) separates perceptual questions appropriately; the [measurement plan](../../MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md#5-condition-matrix-and-data-separation) protects complete held-out conditions. The listening protocol still needs an executable preregistration. This review reports no experiment or standards-conformance result.

Pair each specimen excerpt with a frozen model prediction for the same recorded excitation/events, configuration, and load. Identify repeated physical takes separately. Blind participants and administering experimenters to source identity; hold decoding separately until analysis is locked. Randomize and counterbalance source order and condition allocation within participants; record seeds, training, monitoring, breaks, exclusions, and stimulus hashes.

Absolute-level trials should preserve calibrated voltage relationships through a common playback gain. Matched-timbre trials need a frozen loudness-matching rule and recorded adjustments; their results cannot establish absolute gain fidelity. Keep onset/timing trials separate, with alignment rules that cannot remove the discrepancy being evaluated.

Analyze repeated trials with a prespecified model accounting for crossed participant and stimulus/phrase variation, plus session clustering where applicable. Report independent participant and stimulus counts alongside trial counts. Plan sample size using pilot-informed clustering and a justified smallest relevant effect; adding trials cannot substitute automatically for independent listeners or excerpts. Report condition-level estimates and uncertainty, including failed or inconclusive conditions.

**Preregistration gate.** Before recruiting confirmatory listeners or opening final test outcomes, archive a timestamped, hashed protocol specifying the participant population, paired stimulus manifest, endpoints, contrasts, randomization, exclusions, stopping rule, analysis model, missing-data handling, and simulation-based power/sensitivity. Freeze equivalence margins and confidence level with justification. Require the appropriate interval within those margins, or a discrimination upper bound below the declared limit; nonsignificance alone cannot pass. Prespecify multiplicity handling across register, velocity, load, and perceptual endpoints. Restrict conclusions to the tested population, stimuli, monitoring, and conditions; never infer universal indistinguishability.
