# A013 — Modal normalization and physical units

**Finding:** [NUMERICS](../../NUMERICS.md), the [API](../../../include/rhodes/research_engine.hpp), and [implementation](../../../src/research_engine.cpp) consistently assign metres, metres/second, and kilograms to independent synthetic modes. Their energy expression is appropriate for that primitive. However, the mass-proportional strike impulse prescribes velocity changes; its weights do not identify physical hammer-force participation. Pickup observation weights correctly remain separate. No specimen mode shapes, effective masses, or coupled-mode identification are established.

**Proposed PHY-002/KEY-001 contract:** Reconstruct physical displacement as $u(r)=\sum_j\phi_j(r)q_j$, with dimensionless shapes and metre-valued coordinates. For each mode, record a nonnodal reference point/direction and normalize its projected shape component to +1. Define $m_j=\int\rho|\phi_j|^2dV$, including lumped contributions, in kilograms. Hammer and damper participation are projections of that same shape at their contact points; use each projection for both force application and contact-motion observation. Pickup observation uses the pickup location/direction. Record equilibrium, mounting, magnetic loading, sign anchors, and uncertainty, as required by [Architecture](../../ARCHITECTURE.md).

**Rescaling test:** Under $q'_j=s_jq_j$, require $\phi'_j=\phi_j/s_j$, $m'_j=m_j/s_j^2$, and force/observation weights divided by $s_j$; generalized impulses also divide by $s_j$. Run a synthetic two-mode finite-hammer reference with $s=(2,-3)$. Require matching physical displacement, contact force/separation, rebound, nonlinear pickup voltage, and total energy within convergence-derived tolerances. Deliberately omit one force-weight transformation and require the comparison to fail. This distinguishes consistent normalization from matching only frequencies or output gain.

**Identification:** Future coupled specimen modes need calibrated force-to-motion responses at multiple locations; output spectra alone cannot separate modal mass, participation, and pickup sensitivity. Retain off-diagonal damping/coupling when identification requires it and evaluate $E=(\dot q^TM\dot q+q^TKq)/2$, plus contact energy. First add the normalization contract to PHY-002 assets; carry it across KEY-001 construction zones.

**Verification:** Source inspection using `cat`, `sed`, and `rg`; no numerical or physical tests run. Only this report changed.
