# A059 — Suitcase optical and thermal acquisition histories

**Boundary.** The [measurement plan](../../MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md) reports no specimen or calibrated acquisitions; [A024](A024.md) supplies a proposed optical/thermal protocol, not measured parameters. These histories belong to revision-specific `CIR-001` Suitcase profiles after Stage validation. Optical tracking of hammer/tine displacement measures mechanics; it does not identify modulation-emitter or LDR memory.

**Proposed acquisition.** Identify installed emitter technology, board revision, component designators, replacements, optical geometry and shielding before selecting a model. Retain incandescent heating/light dynamics separately from LED drive-to-light behavior; do not assign filament lag to LEDs. Synchronously capture drive voltage/current, light incident at each LDR, individual LDR conductance, channel gains, supply, ambient temperature and observable device temperatures. Compare drive-to-light trajectories with light-to-conductance trajectories to distinguish emitter lag from detector history and channel mismatch.

Record dark and illuminated preconditioning, duration, warm-up, initial temperature, repeated steps/pulses, recovery and interruptions as complete histories. Preserve circuit state across note retriggers. Characterize optical sensor bandwidth, wavelength response and geometry relative to the LDR; a scalar light reading alone cannot resolve changing spectral illumination. Document clock offset/drift, channel gain/phase, filtering and probe loading with calibration validity and uncertainty.

**Validation and ingestion.** Extend the [acquisition contract](../../specs/DATA_INGESTION.md) with immutable raw histories, calibrated units, configuration/source hashes and derivation ancestry. Reserve complete preconditioning/temperature/drive sequences and independent sessions for validation and locked final test; crops inherit their parent partition. Compare multirate updates and interpolation against a converged coupled reference, refining optical/thermal steps and audio rates; evaluate envelope, phase, sidebands and recovery with numerical error separated from measurement uncertainty.

**Verification.** Documentary review only; no acquisition, code/state edits, Git operations or broad tests. Next: define the revision-specific history manifest and pilot protocol before acquisition.
