# A016 — Pickup flux, sign, and units

**Assessment.** [Architecture](../../ARCHITECTURE.md), [numerics](../../NUMERICS.md),
the [header](../../../include/rhodes/research_engine.hpp), and
[implementation](../../../src/research_engine.cpp) consistently treat lambda as
flux linkage, with turns included. Polynomial coefficient units are
Wb-turn/m, Wb-turn/m², and Wb-turn/m³. Header argument names omit “turn,”
although their comment resolves the meaning; future asset fields should make
linkage versus per-turn flux explicit.

`PolynomialPickup::voltage` analytically implements
`e = -(a1 + 2*a2*x + 3*a3*x*x)*velocity`, the scalar form of
`-grad(lambda)·velocity`. `ResearchEngine::sample` applies identical
dimensionless observation weights to displacement and velocity, sums modes
before conversion, and therefore retains nonlinear cross terms. The existing
derivative and intermodulation tests support the intended contract by inspection;
they were not run here. Adding constant linkage lambda0 cannot affect motional
voltage. Omitting it is harmless for this source, but identifies neither static
magnetic attraction nor reciprocal force.

**Resolve before MAG-001.** Define positive physical displacement, winding
orientation, named terminals A/B, positive current, and the mapping between the
chosen negative source derivative and `v = R*i + d(lambda)/dt`. The minus sign
alone cannot establish specimen polarity. The network presently assumes aligned
source orientations. Preserve separate reference planes for open-circuit coil
EMF, loaded harp voltage, and the future Stage jack.

**Proposed fixture.** Declare synthetic N=200 turns and per-turn
`phi(x)=phi0+(0.001 Wb/m)*x`; configure linkage coefficients `(0.2,0,0)`.
With x=0.001 m and velocity=0.02 m/s, require `V(A)-V(B)=-0.004 V`;
reversing velocity or leads reverses the sign. Zero velocity and changing phi0
must leave EMF zero and unchanged, respectively. Put that source at key 4,
retain all branches, and use Rb=180 ohms, RL=1425 ohms: loaded harp voltage
must be -0.000666666666666667 V within 1e-12 V. Extend cubic derivative
checks over decreasing finite-difference steps.
