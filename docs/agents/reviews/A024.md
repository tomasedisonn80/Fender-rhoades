# A024 — Optional optical and thermal state review

**Boundary.** The [architecture](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) and [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) correctly reserve these mechanisms for separately identified `CIR-001` Suitcase profiles after the one-note gate. They are outside the first Stage scope. The [research brief](../../research/USER_RESEARCH.txt), sections 6–8, supplies modeling proposals, not specimen measurements.

**Model distinction.** Identify each installed emitter before choosing its law. An incandescent model needs electrical heating, filament temperature, and light output; an LED needs its measured drive-to-light relationship, with temperature dependence where supported. Do not transfer filament lag into an LED profile. Represent each LDR's conductance, unequal rise/fall behavior, and illumination history separately from emitter dynamics. Record ambient temperature and distinguish measured self-heating from assumed thermal behavior. Shared drive, supply, and thermal coupling belong to the connected circuit; individual LDR mismatch remains individual. Note retriggers must not restart these circuit histories.

**One discriminating protocol.** On an identified assembly, repeat calibrated drive steps and pulse sequences after dark and illuminated preconditioning, at recorded temperatures and supply conditions. Synchronously measure emitter voltage/current, spectrally calibrated light output, LDR conductance with documented probe loading, and both channel gains. Comparing drive-to-light and light-to-conductance trajectories separates source lag from detector memory; repeated identical illumination after different histories tests whether a memoryless or symmetric response is adequate. Reserve complete preconditioning/temperature sequences for validation.

**Missing future contract.** Specify component reference designators, board revision, source hashes, parameter units, provenance class, uncertainty, validity domains, state ownership, initialization/warm-up, persistence, and reset semantics. Define bounded multirate updates and state-consistent interpolation. Compare gain envelopes, channel phase, sidebands, and recovery against a tightly converged coupled reference while refining optical/thermal steps; allocate numerical error separately from measurement uncertainty.
