# A015 — Small components and shared supports

**Finding:** [Architecture](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) correctly represents springs, screws, grommets, and supports through observable mass, compliance, loss, and preload. The [inventory](../../../profiles/mark-i-stage-73/components.json) separates tuning-spring mass, grommet compliance, and shared harp coupling, while leaving values unknown. Screw settings and support conditions lack explicit inventory records. The [current kernel](../../NUMERICS.md) has independent synthetic modes; it supplies no calibrated evidence for these interactions.

**Concrete refinement:** Add one versioned mechanical-boundary record referenced by each modal asset and acquisition condition. Record spring mass and position, screw setting, measured grommet compression or preload where available, grommet condition, support constraints, equilibrium, and pickup/load configuration, with SI units and uncertainty. Do not equate screw torque with measured preload. Declare whether each effect is already embedded in assembled modal mass/stiffness/loss or belongs to an explicit attachment/support model. Assign shared harp coordinates once across keys; attaching an extra support oscillator to modes already fitted with that support can count the same resonance twice. Add separate part dynamics only when residuals and independent observations justify them.

**Identification and next gate:** Pitch and decay alone cannot identify individual spring mass, mount stiffness, and damping uniquely. Extend [mechanical acquisition](../../MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md) with calibrated force and simultaneous tine, tonebar, and harp motion; vary one documented spring-position or preload condition and reserve another whole condition for prediction. Until hardware exists, use a synthetic two-resonator/shared-support mass–stiffness–damping reference. Compare its directly assembled transfer functions with an independently reduced model over a declared band, including support motion and transfer between resonators. Require convergence, passive unforced energy decay, and matching poles/residues within predeclared numerical tolerances; deliberately duplicate the support contribution and require the comparison to fail. This is algorithmic evidence only.

**Verification:** Inspected repository documents, inventory, and implementation with `cat`, `sed`, and `rg`. No numerical or physical tests run; only this report changed.
