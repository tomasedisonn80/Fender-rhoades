# A055 — Geometry, loading, and spatial identification

**Inspected:** `AGENTS.md`, project context/state, [measurement plan](../../MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md), [transduction contract](../../specs/TRANSDUCTION_NETWORK.md), and [contact contract](../../specs/CONTACT_REFERENCE.md). These specify future identification; no specimen measurements were inspected. The contracts correctly distinguish linkage derivatives from static attraction and require magnetic/support effects embedded in modes to be declared.

**Proposed discriminatory measurements:** With support and regulation fixed, perturb measured pickup gap and orientation independently, return to baseline, and repeat synchronized native strikes and small-amplitude ringdowns. Record equilibrium, local motion, voltage, and branch current. Compare an electrically isolated open coil with documented loads; an open harp terminal alone does not exclude circulating currents. Independently acquire calibrated quasi-static force/displacement curves to constrain zero-current attraction and magnetic stiffness. Compare a magnet-withdrawn configuration only when mechanical boundary changes are measured and controlled.

For every modal fit, identify the equilibrium, geometry, load, and attached apparatus. Either identify mechanics excluding explicitly modeled magnetic contributions, or retain measured effective modes and apply only a consistently derived change from their reference magnetic condition. Adding full static-force curvature, preload, or electromagnetic damping to already loaded modes double counts those effects.

Compare noncontact motion measurements before and after adding reflective targets or contact sensors; record added mass, attachment, cable forces, and resulting pole/decay changes. Measure spatial force-to-motion transfers at contact, pickup, tonebar, and support locations, with calibrated excitation and common phase reference. A mode weak at the pickup can remain relevant to contact; distinguish pickup observation from contact participation while preserving contact reciprocity.

**Acceptance gates:** Before fitting, derive tolerances from calibration uncertainty and repeated baselines. Require bounded apparatus influence, identified spatial participation, and simultaneous prediction of equilibrium, modal shifts/decays, motion, and loaded voltage on held-out whole geometry/load conditions. Unsupported separation remains unidentified.

**Unrun:** All proposed acquisitions, fits, and acceptance checks; no hardware or software tests executed.
