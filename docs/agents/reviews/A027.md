# A027 — Independent reference convergence audit

**Finding.** [NUMERICS](../../NUMERICS.md) documents analytic free propagation; [contact](../../specs/CONTACT_REFERENCE.md) remains a specification. [VALIDATION](../../VALIDATION.md) requires independent refinements but leaves their stopping rule pending. A small nonlinear residual establishes discrete-equation satisfaction, not timestep accuracy. Bounded, passive trajectories can still accumulate phase error, excessive damping, incorrect rebound or missed brief contacts. Energy balance alone cannot exclude these failures.

**Proposed evidence table.** Freeze fixtures, parameter/domain hashes, methods, precision, compiler, commands, observation times and thresholds before experiments. Tabulate each condition: grazing, stiff impacts, recontact and moving-tine retriggers:

| Sweep | Settings and controls | Recorded evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Timestep | `h,h/2,h/4,h/8`; tight fixed solve/event tolerances | Successive differences; observed order where smooth |
| Nonlinear solve | `tau,tau/10,tau/100`; fixed finest timestep/event tolerance | Maximum scaled residual, iterations, failures; observable changes |
| Events | Bracket widths `eta,eta/2,eta/4`; fixed finest timestep/solve tolerance | Event counts/order, onset/separation times, impulse and rebound |
| Independence | Separate integrator/implementation; analytic separated-motion limit | Matched-time trajectory and energy discrepancies |

Every row reports displacement, velocity, integrated impulse, rebound, waveform, event-time errors, and cumulative energy residual including branch-specific losses and external work. Compare physical timestamps without shifting away onset error. Predeclare per-observable SI limits `A_j + R_j*S_j`, fixed positive scales `S_j`, and energy scale `E_*`; scale equation residuals separately by their units. Preserve raw errors near zero.

**Proposed stop rule.** Accept each fixture only after two successive timestep refinements remain below its budget with decreasing differences; two tolerance tightenings and event refinements each change observables by less than one-tenth that budget; event sequences agree; independent-method and energy discrepancies pass. Record nonsmooth order loss explicitly. Treat plateaus, grazing ambiguity or iteration caps as inconclusive, never convergence.
