# A093 — Cross-system units and signs

**Finding:** No dimensional or sign inconsistency found in the scoped
[numerical contract](../../NUMERICS.md),
[API](../../../include/rhodes/research_engine.hpp),
[implementation](../../../src/research_engine.cpp), and future reference contracts.
This is static inspection, not a coupled-system validation result.

**Mechanics:** Modal displacement is metres, velocity metres/second, mass
kilograms; observation and participation weights are dimensionless.
`J=m*(0.06 m/s)*I*b` has newton-second units, and `delta_v=J/m` agrees with code.
The [contact contract](../../specs/CONTACT_REFERENCE.md) uses
`delta=x_h-b_h^T*q-g0`, hammer force `-F`, and modal force `b_h*F`:
their combined power is `-F*delta_dot`. Reusing that participation vector
preserves reciprocity. Under `q'=s*q`, participation becomes `b/s` and mass
`m/s^2`, preserving physical motion, energy and work. Distinct strike and pickup
weights describe different observations; the current impulse surrogate does not
implement reciprocal contact.

**Transduction:** Linkage is V·s, with polynomial coefficients V·s/m^n;
turn count is included once. The
[magnetic contract](../../specs/TRANSDUCTION_NETWORK.md) explicitly maps
`e=-d(lambda)/dt` to the opposite orientation from passive open-circuit terminal
voltage. With `v=V_A-V_B` and current entering A, `v=R*i+d(lambda)/dt`
and a terminating load obeys `v=-R_L*i`. Its co-energy yields
`F=-U0'+i*lambda0'+L'*i^2/2`, `E_mag=U0+L*i^2/2`, and
`partial F/partial i=partial lambda/partial q`. Zero-current force is `-U0'`;
linkage derivatives cannot identify it. Embedded magnetic stiffness/preload must
not be counted twice.

**Timing and boundary:** Successful processing observes `t_n`, then leaves
mechanics at `t_n+h`; strikes affect the first same-time sample. The
[solver contract](../../specs/REFERENCE_SOLVERS.md) requires matched physical
times and separate event-work rows. Output is synthetic loaded-harp volts,
distinct from a measured Stage jack. Current mechanics omit magnetic reaction;
proposed reciprocal fixtures, measured polarity, specimen wiring and static-force
identification remain future work.

**Verification/next step:** Read-only `cat`/`rg` inspection; no executable tests
run. Freeze terminal orientation and coordinate normalization in the first
coupled synthetic fixture before implementing its energy ledger.
