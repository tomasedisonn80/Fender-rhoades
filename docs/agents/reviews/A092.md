# A092 — Numerical failure recovery

**Contract.** Static inspection of the [API](../../../include/rhodes/research_engine.hpp), [implementation](../../../src/research_engine.cpp), and [numerical contract](../../NUMERICS.md) agrees: invalid notes and failed preparation are atomic; arithmetic failure during `process` is deliberately not. `sample` advances modes while building pickup observations, so a later failure can retain a mixture of old and advanced modes. Earlier successful output remains; the failed sample and every remaining element of the caller span are explicitly zeroed. Clearing uses bounded storage but work proportional to span length: the core has no frame cap or deadline bound.

**Recovery.** Call `reset()` or complete a successful `prepare(rate)` before resuming. Reset retains rate and restores resting mechanics, released keys/pedal, and released damping; preparation additionally rebuilds transitions. A rejected prepare preserves even a previously faulted trajectory, and `all_notes_off()` is not recovery. No latched fault prevents further processing, so caller adherence matters.

**Material gap.** [Primitive tests](../../../tests/cpp/research_engine_test.cpp) and [regressions](../../../tests/cpp/review_regression.cpp) cover invalid-input atomicity, healthy reset silence, and accepted preparation, but do not induce an engine arithmetic fault or check prefix retention, suffix clearing, partially advanced state, and subsequent recovery. Add a deterministic, bounded fault-injection fixture at sample 17 of 64, checking buffer canaries and comparing post-reset/reprepare strike/release behavior with a fresh engine. Avoid enormous retrigger loops.

**Documentation correction.** The [runtime specification](../../specs/RUNTIME_AND_ASSETS.md) places “Diagnostics use bounded status and counters” under “Current verified boundary,” although the exported processing result is only `bool` and no counters exist. Label that sentence a future requirement. Failing-sample/subsystem/reset-required fields and host event rejection remain proposed facilities.

**Validation.** Source/test inspection only; no executable tests run.
