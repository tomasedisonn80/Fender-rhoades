# A051 — Whole-condition held-out design

**Inspection:** Read the [measurement plan](../../MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md),
[validation contract](../../VALIDATION.md), [data contract](../../../data/README.md),
[A046](A046.md), and required operating/project context using `cat` and `sed`.
Used `rg --files` and `git status --short` for inventory. No tests or physical
experiments ran; A046's implementation findings were not independently rechecked.

**Finding:** The plans correctly reserve entire conditions and a later,
independently re-established session. A046 identifies missing shared-group and
derivation metadata. A condition ID alone cannot establish the intended
independence or distinguish factor generalization from session drift.

**Recommended design:** Define conditions by specimen/configuration revision,
physical key, measured strike-speed group, full retrigger/release/pedal sequence,
measured pickup pose, and load/cable/output boundary. Keep complete takes,
phrases, acquisition groups, and their derived artifacts together. Randomize
strike order within feasible acquisition blocks and repeat baseline conditions
across sessions to quantify drift. Predeclare separate panels holding out strike
groups, pickup poses, and resistive/reactive loads, plus selected combined-factor
conditions; record missing combinations explicitly. The initial gate remains
key-specific. Later keyboard claims require held-out keys around construction
transitions and register extremes, with all observations for each reserved key
excluded from fitting and model selection.

**Proposed acceptance gate:** Before fitting, freeze a campaign manifest containing
factor levels, partition assignments, group/ancestor IDs, and required cell counts.
Before final unblinding, hash the candidate, split, evaluator, exclusions, and
per-condition thresholds justified by pilot uncertainty/repeatability. A proposed
audit must reject cross-partition acquisition/phrase/ancestry groups, reserved
factor levels appearing in fit/development, missing required cells, and changed
frozen assets. Every required condition must meet its predeclared physical,
electrical, and timing criteria; aggregate success cannot conceal failure. Any
validation-driven tuning requires relabeling and a fresh holdout.

**Limitations:** Numerical levels, repetitions, and thresholds remain pending
actual pilot data. No specimen measurements currently support executing this
gate. Metadata consistency cannot certify truthful acquisition or independence.
