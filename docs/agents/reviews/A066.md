# A066 — Repeatability and uncertainty acceptance

**Finding.** [Validation](../../VALIDATION.md), the
[measurement plan](../../MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md),
[ingestion contract](../../specs/DATA_INGESTION.md), and [A049](A049.md)
require calibrated uncertainty and repeatability but leave the numerical decision
rule pending. Physical acceptance remains blocked by missing observations.

**Future report fields.** Record specimen/configuration, condition/session IDs,
independent take counts, acquisition/calibration/model/acceptance-plan hashes,
metric units, estimand (single-strike prediction or condition mean), exclusions,
and within-session/between-session variance estimates with intervals. Re-establish
the setup between sessions; crops are not independent repeats. Record nuisance
conditions: temperature/humidity, warm-up, tuning/regulation, operator, pickup
repositioning, probe/loading, and baseline drift.

Retain calibration covariance, shared reference/clock/load groups, propagation
method, and coverage. Repetition does not average away shared calibration bias.
Unknown correlations require defensible bounding or an inconclusive result.
Separate acquisition uncertainty, fitted-parameter uncertainty, observed
variability, reference discretization error, and runtime-reduction error; record
allocations and avoid counting a source twice. Never inflate uncertainty with
the model discrepancy being tested.

**Predeclared acceptance.** Before final-test access, freeze required cells,
metrics, thresholds `T`, coverage/multiplicity policy, numerical budgets,
guard-band construction, and exclusions. For each nonnegative discrepancy,
report estimate `e` and interval `[L,H]` at declared coverage, incorporating
applicable uncertainty and conservative numerical bounds. Pass only when
`H <= T`; fail when `L > T`; otherwise classify as inconclusive. The acceptance
guard band is `H - e`; thresholds must not expand after seeing results.
Numerical-budget breaches cannot be excused by measurement noise. Missing
required cells or invalid calibration make the gate incomplete. Every required
cell must pass; averaging cannot conceal failures. Store outcome, reason,
unresolved inputs, and next action.

**Verification and handoff.** Source inspection only; no tests or physical
measurements performed. Only this review changed. Next: incorporate these fields
and decision rules into the versioned acceptance-report contract.
