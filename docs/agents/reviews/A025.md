# A025 — Optional acoustic boundary audit

**Boundary.** [Architecture](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) and [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) correctly defer `ACO-001` until a separate scope decision. The current loaded Stage target has no cabinet, room, or microphone obligation. Future profiles should freeze amplifier revision, driver/cabinet configuration, electrical reference planes, and receiver coordinates/orientations.

**Coupling.** The [research brief](../../research/USER_RESEARCH.txt), section 9, requires both electrical loading and mechanical radiation. Couple terminal voltage/current, driver motion, and back EMF where material; retain identified mechanical/thermal history. Cabinet radiation and receiver paths need separate evidence. A downstream response cannot substitute for unvalidated amplifier/load interaction.

**Representation.** “No recorded-note playback” can permit a separately approved, rights-cleared IR; “no recorded responses at runtime” requires procedural or fitted causal models. Record the selected interpretation and operating range. An IR's linear scope cannot establish level-dependent driver accuracy. Stereo requires every source-to-receiver path, including cross paths, with common polarity, phase, and propagation-delay references.

**Missing future field.** Propose `acoustic_paths`: a versioned boundary-manifest field mapping each electrical channel/driver to each receiver, referencing geometry and transfer model, with polarity and `propagation_delay_s` tied to a common time origin. This belongs to a future acoustic interface.

**One gate.** Freeze parameters and predict held-out levels/positions using synchronized calibrated terminal voltage/current, driver motion, and receiver pressure. Require separate terminal impedance/transfer and acoustic gain/phase/delay acceptance, including stereo cross paths, against predeclared uncertainty-based thresholds, following [validation principles](../../VALIDATION.md). Retain common timing; independent channel realignment must not conceal delay errors.

**Verification and handoff.** Documentary reads (`rg`, `cat`, `sed`) only; no tests or physical validation performed. Next: specify this interface and gate when optional `ACO-001` is scoped.
