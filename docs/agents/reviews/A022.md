Experimental combinations need their own nonhistorical identity. Shared algorithms are reusable; parameter values, optical histories, supply states, control tapers, and serialized state require matching model semantics, units, port/load assumptions, and validity domains. Equal control names do not establish compatibility.

**One proposed gate.** Before accepting an immutable configuration, require every asset and preset to match one manifest tuple: `(profile_id, configuration_revision, netlist_hash, output_boundary, parameter_schema_version)`. Reject mismatches without substitution. Demonstrate rejection of a Peterson asset selected under Janus and a Stage-jack preset selected under loaded-harp identity before enabling historical-profile selection.
