# A060 — Optional acoustic coupling and acceptance

**Boundary.** [A025](A025.md), [architecture](../../ARCHITECTURE.md), and the
[roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) preserve `ACO-001` as a separately scoped extension;
the loaded Stage target has no acoustic obligation. Freeze amplifier revision,
driver/cabinet identity, electrical reference planes, and receiver geometry
before selecting representations.

**Coupling.** Require compatible driver force and back-EMF relations, with
terminal voltage/current and mechanical force/velocity signs explicit. Preserve
material suspension, electrical, and thermal history. Cabinet/radiation loading
must enter the mechanical boundary without double counting. A downstream
response alone cannot establish amplifier–driver interaction. For stereo, retain
every driver-to-receiver path, including cross paths and polarity; keep inactive
channels electrically connected during identification.

**Proposed acceptance fixture.** For an approved stereo profile, mount two
calibrated microphones at surveyed coordinates/orientations relative to one
identified cabinet. Record environmental conditions and acquisition latency.
Drive each input separately with calibrated sweeps and two-tone bursts, then
both together. Synchronously capture speaker-terminal voltage/current, driver
velocity, and both pressure signals on a common clock. Freeze fitted parameters;
predict reserved drive levels and displaced receiver positions. Following
[validation](../../VALIDATION.md), predeclare uncertainty-based limits separately
for terminal impedance, mechanical transfer, acoustic gain/phase/delay,
harmonic/intermodulation residuals, and post-burst recovery. Preserve a common
time origin: per-path realignment must not conceal propagation or stereo-delay
errors. Report failures per condition, without averaging them away.

**Runtime scope.** Separate small-signal linear response from excursion-dependent
behavior and thermal compression. The scope decision must distinguish “no
recorded notes” from “no recorded responses at runtime.” A permitted impulse
response requires rights and a declared linear range; stricter execution needs
procedural or fitted causal models. Offline recordings remain identification
evidence, and neither representation establishes level-dependent accuracy alone.

**Verification and handoff.** Documentary review and local report checks only;
no implementation, experiment, or fidelity validation claimed. Next: adopt the
fixture and response policy when `ACO-001` receives its boundary decision.
