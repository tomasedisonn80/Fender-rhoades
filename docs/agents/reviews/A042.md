**Verdict:** No defect found in the scoped DAG logic. Unknown dependencies are
rejected before traversal; the visiting/visited DFS catches self-links and longer
cycles. Every completed task must have completed prerequisites, passing evidence
and every required evidence kind. Reopening checks completed direct dependents;

done, excludes active claims, and sorts by ascending priority then ID. The empty
live selection reflects the active `FND-001` lease, not a hardware-wide blockade.

and `STG-001` are parallel successors of the one-note gate, preserving distinct
loaded-harp and Stage-jack claims. STG requires measurement, numerical and
