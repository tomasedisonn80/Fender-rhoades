# A056 — Circuit operating points and initialization

**Current absence.** The [engine](../../../src/research_engine.cpp) implements a synthetic resistive harp, without powered-device models, rail storage, or nonlinear circuit initialization. `CIR-001` remains future work after `STG-001`; the [architecture](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) and [A023](A023.md)/[A024](A024.md) describe requirements, not validated circuitry.

**Future contract.** Freeze each revision's corrected graph, reference designators, oriented ports, frequency-dependent source impedance, controls, supply impedance, and load. Solve shared signal stages, rail currents, and load feedback together. Device records need model/version hashes, SI parameters, provenance, uncertainty, and voltage/current/temperature/frequency domains; reject unsupported excursions explicitly.

Record capacitor charge, inductor flux, and optical/thermal histories with initialization residuals. Distinguish equilibrium startup from deliberate power-on transients and periodic oscillator operation with declared phase. Specify warm-up, reset, and restoration semantics; musical retriggers preserve circuit states. Preserve the original schematic and each correction's before/after connectivity, rationale, board-photo/continuity evidence, reviewer status, and resulting netlist hash. Unverified corrections remain hypotheses.

**Proposed acceptance fixture.** On one frozen, stable configuration, compare the solved zero-signal equilibrium with an independent long warm-up, then apply positive/negative small state perturbations and verify recovery without unexplained startup energy. Repeat a sustained 700 Hz probe plus a gated 1100 Hz burst under two recorded source impedances and two loads, including a load transition during the burst. Predeclare amplitudes, timing, supply conditions, and allowed domains; capture input/output voltage-current, rail sag, bias recovery, and all stored states. Compare against held-out board measurements when available.

Following [VALIDATION](../../VALIDATION.md), freeze observable budgets before acceptance. Independently refine timestep and nonlinear tolerances; report scaled KCL/KVL residuals, iterations, energy balance, and branch selection from multiple initial guesses. Exhausted Newton/continuation budgets must return explicit failure, never an accepted unconverged state.

**Verification and handoff.** Targeted documentary/source inspection only; fixture unrun. No code, state, or Git changes. Next: establish the revision-specific graph and initialization manifest before implementing `CIR-001`.
