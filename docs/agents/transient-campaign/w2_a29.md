## Independent oracle

The test reads public boundary q/v states and evaluates the damped oscillator
as `q(t) = exp(-beta*t) * (A*cos(wd*t) + B*sin(wd*t))`, with
`A=q(0)` and `B=(v(0)+beta*A)/wd`; velocity is the independently differentiated
amplitude form. It computes frequencies, decay and trigonometry in `long double`,
then combines local modal motion before applying the polynomial pickup. The
full 73-branch resistive load is independently reduced to gain `12000/12019`.
The oracle does not call the prepared matrices, oscillator transition,
polynomial-pickup helper, harp helper, or coefficient generator. Production Q6
nodes are test inputs; their independent derivation is outside this assignment.

The absolute voltage limit is **1e-13 V**, below the frozen 1e-12 V numerical
gate. Constants are the declared synthetic model, not measured Rhodes data.
