## Diagnosis actually observed

The unequal-prelude fixture passed with local GCC 14.2 default target options.
Adding `-march=x86-64-v3 -ffp-contract=fast` reproduced the same assertion:

- 32000 Hz, float64 output, processing reset; first difference at frame 5.
- 32 of 256 frames differed; maximum difference was
  `5.5511151231257827e-17` **host-output/sample units after monitoring gain**.
- All 219 modal states were exactly equal; maximum displacement and velocity
  differences were zero. Cursors were 512 and 256, respectively.
- First samples were `-0x1.e3ed595c2451dp-20` and
  `-0x1.e3ed595c2452p-20`.

The different silent prefixes place otherwise equivalent FIR histories at
different ring positions. Disassembly of the reproducing executable contained
`vfmadd231sd` instructions. With the same x86-64-v3 target and only contraction
changed to `-ffp-contract=off`, the unequal-prelude test passed all 24 reset
scenarios. Matching the control's prelude then passed with contraction enabled
and retained exact comparisons. This establishes a local contraction-sensitive
fixture comparison; native GCC 15.2/MSVC confirmation remains for the next CI run.
