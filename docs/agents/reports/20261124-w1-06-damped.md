## Derivation and verdict

For this synthetic free-mass fixture, relative compression and speed obey
`delta'=w`, `mu*w'=-delta*(k+c*w)`, with `mu=0.0016 kg`, `k=20000 N/m`,
`c=40000 N s/m²`, `delta(0)=0`, and `w(0)=1 m/s`.
Eliminating time and integrating `w/(k+c*w)` gives

`delta²=(2*mu*k/c²)*(Phi(1)-Phi(w))`,
`Phi(w)=alpha*w-log(1+alpha*w)`, `alpha=c/k`.

Differentiating force yields
`F'=(k+c*w)*(w-c*delta²/mu)` on the positive-force branch. Its loading maximum
therefore solves `g(w)=Phi(w)+alpha*w/2-Phi(1)=0`.
Here `g(0)<0`, `g(1)>0`, and
`g'(w)=alpha²*w/(1+alpha*w)+alpha/2>0` for `0<w<1`; the root is unique.
Compression peaks later, at `w=0`. During positive-force rebound `w<0`, both
terms make `F'<0`; a separated/clipped zero cannot exceed the positive maximum.
The contract's force/compression distinction and invariant are correct.

## Implementation cautions

The substitution `w=1-u²` and `r=alpha*u²/(1+alpha)` gives the adopted
`rho` series exactly after division by `u²`. It avoids subtracting nearly equal
invariants at the initial point. Its limit returns
`sqrt(2*mu/(k+c))=0.0002309401076758503`; the separate witness matched it.
For these loading integrals `0<=r<=2/3`, so the series converges. Preserve the
minus sign before the series. Keep `c=0` as a separate analytic branch and use
a stable small-argument evaluation for `x-log1p(x)`.

Retain bisection endpoint signs and speed brackets; a speed bracket is not a
time bracket. Enforce evaluation and wall-time caps during optional timing,
record stagnation/nonfinite/convergence failures, and keep value references
available when SciPy timing is absent. No issue requiring a contract change was
found. This synthetic diagnostic cannot close general continuous-extrema or
measured-fidelity acceptance.
