unchanged coefficient symmetry retains the declared 48-frame group delay without

Successful cells advance the ring once. Invalid cells are fully checked before
any history or ring-position write. Retrigger, release, sustain and all-notes-off
forward to the mechanical owner without clearing convolution history. Hard reset
clears both mechanics and history and reuses the prepared plan. Empty processing
never enters the loop. Candidate preparation remains transactional.

Any failed observation, nonfinite provisional FIR sum, or failed/nonfinite endpoint
advance prevents publication of that frame and zeros its suffix; earlier outputs
remain untouched. A failed advance may have changed mechanics. The wrapper's
standalone caller must explicitly reset; `BasicEventProcessor` supplies the fault
latch and prevents queued reset events or empty blocks from bypassing recovery.
This is preserved behavior, not an all-or-nothing mechanical rollback claim.

The fault fixture covers six node positions, four failure offsets, advance errors,
and FIR overflow against an already nonzero output history, including counters,
frame coordinates, retained prefix, cleared suffix and explicit-reset recovery.

nodes to `nullopt`, so those injections do not independently reach the wrapper's
additional finite-cell scan. No production defect follows from that distinction.
