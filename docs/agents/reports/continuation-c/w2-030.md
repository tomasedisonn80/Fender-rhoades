## Reviewed guarantees

- The 2560-bit integer capacity covers the conservative 1496-bit parsing intermediate and 2547-bit formatting coefficient. Multiplication carries and subtraction borrows fit unsigned 64-bit arithmetic; descending left shifts preserve unread limbs and avoid word-width shifts.
- Exponent saturation cannot admit a nonzero out-of-range decimal: the subsequent decimal-order guard bounds every scaling loop. Zero retains its sign until controller canonicalization.
- Quotient/remainder rounding implements nearest-even directly. A subnormal carry to `2^52` already has the smallest-normal encoding; normal significand carry and the final rounded-value bound correctly handle 32.
- Decimal rounding carry updates the exponent before trailing-zero removal. Scientific output needs at most 24 bytes including a negative sign; fixed notation is shorter. Failed formatting preserves caller output.
- Controller bit-domain checks reject negative subnormals despite DAZ. Gain normalization bypasses values that necessarily quantize to zero; remaining power-of-two scaling stays normal and exact. Public output is staged until success.
- Clang pragmas restore the includer's policy. No floating `from_chars`/`to_chars` runtime dependency remains; this is source-level portability evidence only.
