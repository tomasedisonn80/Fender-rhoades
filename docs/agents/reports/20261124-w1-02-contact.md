## Independent mathematics to implement

For conservative free masses, reduced mass is `mu=0.0016 kg`,
`omega=sqrt(20000/mu)` and the compression maximum is `1/omega` at
`pi/(2*omega)`. Force peaks there at `20000/omega`.

For damped contact, use `alpha=c/k`,
`Phi(w)=alpha*w-log1p(alpha*w)` and
`delta(w)^2=(2*mu*k/c^2)*(Phi(w0)-Phi(w))`. Compression peaks at `w=0`;
force peaks at the unique root of
`Phi(w)+(alpha/2)*w-Phi(w0)=0` on `(0,w0)`. Its derivative is strictly
positive there, and the force decreases on rebound. Thus a dense sampled ODE
maximum is unnecessary for these value references. Keep force and compression
times distinct. Use the contract's bounded bisection/refinement and regularized
time quadrature; preserve the optional SciPy boundary.

Forced clamp has maximum compression `1e-4 m` at zero and identically zero
enabled hammer force over the full duration. Disabled damper records are a
different semantic case. For rounded `preload_equilibrium`, recompute the exact
rational perturbation-energy enclosure and branch margins in
[assignment 54](../transient-campaign/w3_a54.md). Positive damper values have
certified enclosures, not a mathematically exact positive plateau; their
maximizer time remains unresolved. Enabled separated hammer zero plateaus do
have full-duration maximizer intervals.
