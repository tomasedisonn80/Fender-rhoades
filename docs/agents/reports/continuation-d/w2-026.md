## Implementation

Added `tests/cpp/vst3_expression_audio_test.cpp`, compiled separately for models
1/direct, 2/filtered and 3/quadrature. Every selector exercises float and double
at 48 kHz:

- `default_queues`: default unity and held quarter-expression with null, empty,
  absent-expression and present-empty-expression queues.
- `anchor_duplicates`: offset-minus-one anchor, raw duplicate ramp endpoints,
  held continuation, and one-frame zero/unity endpoints.
- `combined_curves`: independent gain/expression curves in both queue orders.
- `nondyadic_curve`: predetermined canonical `.1`/`.9` anchors and an audible
  witness against prematurely narrowing the interpolated control.
- `muted_resume`: continued held mechanics, muted strikes/releases and sustain,
  exact future filter continuation, and nonzero released tails after unmuting.
- `flush_recall`: duplicate zero-frame controls, unchanged time/motion, matching
  recalled histories, recalled interpolation anchors, final flush precedence,
  and legacy-unity recall after nonunity.
- `partition_curves`: W1-017's exact 32-frame dyadic curves, `[1,2,4,1,8,16]`
  and `[8,8,8,8]` delivery, remapped events, final state and subsequent tails.

A local three-queue container preserves raw duplicates. The independent curve
uses complete point maps and long-double barycentric weights. Expected output
uses a double unity-expression peer, applies expression, then converts once to
the target format. Combined controls additionally recover normal-domain volts
from a unity-gain peer by exact division by 32, preserving legacy multiplication
order. Dyadic nonzero outputs and unity are bit-exact; muted samples require
numerical zero. Non-dyadic bounds remain 16 double epsilons times the monitored
magnitude, or one float ULP, without an absolute floor. Each accepted block
checks stereo bytes, finite output, guard samples, silence flags, canonical
live/published/saved controls, cursor and independently tracked ownership.
