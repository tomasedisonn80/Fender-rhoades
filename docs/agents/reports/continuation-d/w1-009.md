## Fixed-capacity fixture traps and coverage additions

- `tests/cpp/vst3_test_support.hpp:99–107` has only two queue pointers. It is a
  host fixture capacity, not an asserted production limit. Keep it for legacy
  gain/sustain fixtures and introduce a separate three-control/19-queue fixture,
  or enlarge it deliberately. Enlarging it also requires replacing the explicit
  `std::array<...,2>` conditional assignment in
  `vst3_automation_contract_test.cpp:124–127`; brace assignments elsewhere can
  remain two populated entries with count 2. Its scalar oracle at `:149–164`
  accepts only gain/pedal and must not silently classify Expression as sustain.
- `tests/cpp/vst3_command_test_support.hpp:34` has capacity 18. If widened to 19,
  `vst3_command_atomicity_test.cpp:513–528` must not keep iterating
  `ascending.values.size()` against the 18-entry `order` and `17 - index` reverse
  expression: that creates out-of-bounds access/unsigned underflow. Either keep
  the old fixture loop bounded by its declared count or add Expression and a
  complete 19-entry permutation.
- Full old-capacity coverage is at
  `vst3_command_bounds_test.cpp:213–263` (18 queues/read counts and command offset
  2), `vst3_host_test.cpp:1108–1139` (allocation wrapper and 18 queue objects), and
  `vst3_command_partition_test.cpp:450`. These remain valid legacy workloads;
  extend or supplement with all 19 queues and Expression to claim the new maximum.
  Keep 256 raw commands/events and the physical-action limit unchanged. In the
  bounds fixture, inserting Expression also shifts the early aggregate-overflow
  getter count currently asserted as 4 at `:252`.
- Existing local two-object arrays in `vst3_host_test.cpp:282,340,555` and
  `vst3_buffer_contract_test.cpp:308` do not need expansion for their existing
  gain/sustain scenarios. `apps/vst3_benchmark.cpp:176–181` is likewise a fixed
  two-control workload; changing it changes retained benchmark comparability.
- `tests/cpp/vst3_test_support.hpp:133–140` loads state through the legacy encoder.
  Leave that loader explicitly legacy-only or add an adaptive/version-2 loader;
  otherwise new non-unity-expression recalls will fail in the fixture itself.
  Its 64-byte stream storage already fits both formats. The 16-byte transfer
  chunk default at `:121` is valid fragmentation, not a 16-byte capacity limit.
