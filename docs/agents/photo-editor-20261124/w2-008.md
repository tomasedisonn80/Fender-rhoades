## Actual observations

`probe.log` reports three discovered FX, three successful insertions at index
zero on separate tracks, and `loaded_count=3`. The discovered identifiers were
the requested absolute bundle paths. `/proc/<reaper-pid>/maps`, retained in
`loaded-maps.txt`, independently shows all three corresponding native modules.

| --- | --- |
| VST3i: Rhodes Research Prototype (Rhodes Research) | `f92e77ce95ca863c9ea84a8d42eb191d64b553e38a4c899dbc8007a2e6ef31e3` |
| VST3i: Rhodes Research Filtered (Rhodes Research) | `48d14e144c483140e1a442e4179e16c269757b68f8159dff241112fe12747c6a` |
| VST3i: Rhodes Research Quadrature (Rhodes Research) | `e3653d10c71a4087cc120d73c845a9d0db99acbd9904a33068e850874089f7a7` |

REAPER returned 27 parameters for each FX; this worker did not enumerate their
names or distinguish host-added controls, so this is not a claim that the
plug-in itself exposes 27 native parameters.

The Filtered floating editor handle remained non-null at creation and after
two seconds. Its visible X11 hierarchy contained a 1024×796 REAPER floating
window, a 1024×768 child at y=28, and the native 1024×768 photo child. The latter
was captured and visually inspected: the full photograph, keyboard, cabinet,
legs, background and pedal are present. No exact pixel-equality claim is made:
the bundled source is 1536×1152 while the captured editor is 1024×768.

Three launches were used: initial scan and dialog inspection, a bounded
dialog-inspection follow-up, and the final automated native-child capture.
The earlier evidence is preserved under `attempt1/` and `attempt2/`.
The final runner sent Escape to the explicitly named `Error opening devices`
dialog, then positioned/raised the floating editor. The desktop root capture
still shows the main window above much of the editor under bare Xvfb. The
native-child capture is complete; do not present the root capture as an
unobscured desktop screenshot.

The first launch displayed the normal evaluation countdown. Later launches
did not display it during their bounded captures. No evaluation metadata was
edited by the worker. Startup logged a JACK connection failure, and REAPER's
main window showed `[audio device closed]`. There is no device or sound pass.

The process was alive at capture and was then explicitly terminated by the
runner (`exit_before_cleanup=null`, cleanup exit `-15`). The wrapper returned
0. This is controlled teardown, not evidence of graceful session shutdown.
The Lua script wrote `startup-three-variants.rpp`; reopening it was not tested.
