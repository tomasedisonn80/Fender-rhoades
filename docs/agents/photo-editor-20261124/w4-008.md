## Review conclusions

- Event consumption is capped at 64 per poll; reaching the cap cancels conservatively. An incomplete handoff gets at most one additional ordering fence. Queued genuine loss remains sticky through a subsequent focus gain.
- Cold acquisition requires the recorded Pointer/Nonlinear sequence and an exact parent/top completion. It expires on the first synchronized accepted editor acquisition, on loss, or after handoff. Asynchronous acquisition deliberately persists across idle drains, matching the retained causal trace.
- Reparent cancels and requests one same-window refresh; destroy cancels without requesting repeated stale-handle attachment. Subscriptions remain on independent XCB connections, preserving the SDK client's masks and queue.
- Runner startup/readiness/test waits are bounded; child/server use separate process groups, and finalization targets both groups on ordinary completion, timeout, and handled cancellation. Missing tools fail. The test compiles the actual helper directly; its body remains the retained fixture.

The upward Ancestor/Virtual/Inferior sequence agrees with the [X11 focus-event specification](https://xorg.freedesktop.org/releases/current/doc/xproto/x11protocol.html). [XEmbed §3.2](https://specifications.freedesktop.org/xembed/latest-single/) separates logical focus and X focus; this implementation remains an observed ancestor-handoff workaround.
