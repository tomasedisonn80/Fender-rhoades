# W4-A061 — Fail-closed contact-auditor inputs

## Change and boundary

Hardened `tools/validate_contact_reference.py` and added
`tests/python/test_contact_auditor_validation.py`. A pure
`validate_run_input()` checks candidate data before constructing an oracle,
indexing modal vectors, sorting refinement controls or copying events into JSON.
Controls require finite positive numbers; vectors require exactly two finite
components; acceleration/scalar exports reject non-finite values and boolean
coercion. Magnitude diagnostics reject negatives. Event, snapshot and container
shapes are checked explicitly. Unknown numeric extensions cannot carry NaN/Inf
into serialized results.

Malformed evidence produces explicit failure. Empty valid coverage remains
incomplete; `success=false` retains its failure even without samples. Missing
optional diagnostics and independent extrema remain incomplete. Narrow checks
also reject derived physics, energy-ledger and power-residual overflow without
emitting non-finite metrics. No broad exception suppression was added.

The frozen physics, budgets and missing-extrema gate are unchanged. AST comparison
against the prior committed auditor confirmed identical definitions for 13
numerical functions/types and eight frozen constants. This is input hardening,
not new physical acceptance or a repair of A054's refinement failures.
