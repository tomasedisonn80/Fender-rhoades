# A068 — Finite-rate aliasing observations

## Procedure

Built the unchanged renderer with GCC 14.2, C++20, strict warnings and `-O3` on
Linux x86-64. Fifteen one-second float32 WAV renders cover MIDI 48, 89, 90, 96,
100 at 48/96/192 kHz, velocity 0.7 and monitoring gain 1. Sample `n` observes
state at `n/fs`, before advancement. Direct aligned decimation differs by at
most `6.53e-10` relative RMS over the inspected windows; no alignment fitting
or one-sample shift was applied.

Using NumPy 2.3.5/SciPy 1.17.0, centered Kaiser-14 FIRs use 4097/8193 taps at
192 kHz and 2049/4097 at 96 kHz. Cutoffs are the destination Nyquist frequency;
response grids report passband through cutoff−500 Hz and stopband from
cutoff+500 Hz. The maximum half-support is 21.333 ms. Impulse checks verify
centering. The [firwin](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy-1.17.0/reference/generated/scipy.signal.firwin.html)
cutoff is a half-amplitude point; [same convolution](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy-1.17.0/reference/generated/scipy.signal.fftconvolve.html)
retains boundary effects.

| 48 | −153.05 | −152.81 | −154.83 |

For MIDI 100/48 kHz, early residual RMS/peak are `5.776e-10`/`1.103e-9` synthetic
volts. A preselected 19,467.439 Hz damped sinusoid—the predicted fold of the
28,532.561 Hz quadratic component—explains 97.30% of residual squared norm.
Its extrapolated amplitude is `1.349e-9 V`, 0.55% above the independently derived
synthetic coefficient; the 96 kHz control fit is only `5.89e-12 V`.

Post-edge filter-length sensitivity is at most `4.09e-9` relative RMS; changing
the 48 kHz reference from 96 to 192 kHz changes it by at most `1.35e-8`.
The 192→96 kHz filter preserves the highest ideal carrier, 42.799 kHz,
with gain `0.9999999973`. MIDI 90's 24.020 kHz carrier lies in the 48 kHz filter
transition, so its amplitude cannot be inferred from this comparison.

## Limits and retained evidence

The edge-sensitive 0–20 ms onset differs by approximately −34/−40 dB at
48/96 kHz and is reference-rate sensitive. Zero extension, the impulsive onset,
filter transition and float32 export effects prevent an alias-only interpretation.
Post-edge residuals remain below the conservative one-ULP export envelope.
This supports one folded-line observation, not audibility, a certified bound,
full-keyboard convergence, physical fidelity or RT-001 completion.
