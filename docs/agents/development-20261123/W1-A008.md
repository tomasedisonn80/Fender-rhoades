## Ledger and clipping

For positive compression, ordinary loss is `c*delta^p*delta_dot^2`; when `k+c*delta_dot<=0`, force is zero and loss is **`-k*delta^p*delta_dot`**. That branch has decreasing compression, hence nonnegative loss. Retain its stored potential until separation. Never evaluate fractional powers on negative gaps.

Include modal loss and damper work `P=-F_d*ell_dot`, evaluated at actual stage times. Keep ordinary/clamp loss channels separate. Compute endpoint energy independently, then `r=Delta E+D-W`; retain cumulative residual and sum of absolute interval residuals. Never manufacture loss from the residual. The frozen forced-clamp oracle requires `0.0001 J`, whereas the wrong unclipped expression gives `0.0002 J`.

For discrete-gradient midpoint, define `Gbar=Delta U/Delta delta`, `wbar=Delta delta/h`, and `Fbar=max(0,Gbar+Bbar*wbar)`, with consistent `Bbar>=0`. Contact loss `h*(Fbar-Gbar)*wbar` is nonnegative; compatible moving-damper work is `-Fbar*Delta ell`. This balance can still smear force across separation and shift rebound/phase. Damped contact remains nonlinear even at `p=1`.
