## Findings

Unfiltered aligned relative RMS reproduces `6.52880905545e−10`. This supports the
`n/fs` observation convention; direct decimation also folds high-frequency
carriers, so close agreement cannot demonstrate absence of aliasing.

An independent normalized sinc×Kaiser construction differs from the design API
by at most `1.735e−18` per coefficient. Direct complex DTFT evaluation reproduces
reported carrier gains. Centered odd FIR support reaches 21.333 ms; the 25–50 ms
and 150–500 ms windows exclude onset and the 650 ms release. The half-amplitude
cutoff and finite transition prevent treating this reference as an ideal
brick-wall filter, especially for MIDI 90's 24.020 kHz carrier.

For MIDI 100/48 kHz, replay gives early residual RMS `5.77633e−10 V`, peak
`1.10262e−9 V`, and **−136.515637 dB relative to reference RMS**. Independently
expanding the differentiated quadratic modal term predicts 28,532.561239 Hz,
folding to 19,467.438761 Hz. Explicit two-column normal equations reproduce the
`1.348653e−9 V` extrapolated fitted amplitude and 97.3027% explained squared norm;
the independent predicted amplitude is `1.341311e−9 V`. The same-frequency 96 kHz
control yields `5.89242e−12 V`.

Onset differences reproduce −33.735/−40.096 dB at 48/96 kHz and include zero-padding,
impulse and filtering effects. Selected post-edge residuals lie below their
conservative one-ULP export envelopes. Those envelopes exclude core/analysis
roundoff; neither they nor the selected fit establish a certified alias bound,
confidence interval, audibility or specimen fidelity.
