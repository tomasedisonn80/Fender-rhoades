## Concrete findings

**P2 — control meaning depends on flush modes.** Under DAZ, negative subnormal
note values are accepted by engine, tracker, event and host paths. Positive tiny
notes become releases: existing holders drop from one to zero, and engine
continuation switches to released damping. Nonzero subnormal payloads pass
exact-zero-only event checks; a reset clears ringing energy and advances the
previous cursor from one to two instead of rejecting atomically.

Under FTZ or DAZ, normalized double gain `1e-40` loses its expected binary32
encoding `0x000116c2` during controller/automation conversion. A valid state
recall preserves that encoding through a zero-frame flush in **all four modes**,
but a subsequent four-frame call with no notes or automation changes both live
and published gain to zero. This isolates the remaining control/render issue
from the repaired codec.

Use bit-domain validation and actual-zero predicates across control boundaries;
make binary32 quantization/widening independent of flush modes and preserve
configuration separately from DSP underflow. This report does not classify tiny
audio output underflow as a defect, or claim DAW/non-x86 coverage.
