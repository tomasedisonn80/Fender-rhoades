# W1-A017 — Cubic pickup bandwidth audit

**Scope:** documentary/analytic review for RT-001; synthetic parameters only.
No DSP changes, audio-error measurements, or acceptance promotion.

## Derivation

Sources: `src/research_engine.cpp` (`frequency_ratios`, `prepare`,
`PolynomialPickup::voltage`, `sample`), `include/rhodes/research_engine.hpp`
(rate limits and nonzero polynomial coefficients), and
`docs/specs/REFERENCE_SOLVERS.md` (nonlinear bandwidth and resampling).

For MIDI 100,

\[
f_0=440\,2^{(100-69)/12}=2637.020455\ \mathrm{Hz},\qquad
(f_1,f_2,f_3)=f_0(1,2.73,5.41).
\]

Thus the nominal modal frequencies are 2637.020, 7199.066, and
14266.281 Hz. The implementation uses damped carrier frequencies
\(\sqrt{(2\pi f_i)^2-\beta_i^2}/(2\pi)\); these are slightly lower.
Modal observations are summed before

\[
u=-\frac{d}{dt}(a_1x+a_2x^2+a_3x^3)
  =-(a_1+2a_2x+3a_3x^2)\dot x.
\]

For stationary sinusoidal modes, potential carrier locations are

\[
\mathcal F=\bigcup_{p=1}^{3}
\left\{\left|\sum_{j=1}^{p}s_jf_{i_j}\right|:
s_j\in\{-1,1\},\ i_j\in\{1,2,3\}\right\}.
\]

Indices may repeat: this includes intermodulation, not just harmonics.
Differentiation preserves nonzero carrier locations; stationary DC vanishes.
Quadratic and cubic maxima are respectively **28532.561 Hz** and
**42798.842 Hz**. Decay envelopes and strike/release/retrigger events make
actual trajectories non-bandlimited; these maxima bound ideal carrier locations.

## Rate comparison

For a line at frequency \(f\), its sampled location is
\(A(f)=|((f+F_s/2)\bmod F_s)-F_s/2|\).

| Rate (Hz) | Nyquist (Hz) | Location of \(2f_3\) (Hz) | Location of \(3f_3\) (Hz) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 32000 | 16000 | 3467.439 | 10798.842 |
| 48000 | 24000 | 19467.439 | 5201.158 |
| 96000 | 48000 | 28532.561, unfolded | 42798.842, unfolded |

At 32 kHz, the top mode passes the implementation's \(0.45F_s=14400\) Hz
guard, despite nonlinear folding. Preserving every ideal cubic carrier requires
\(F_s>6f_3=85597.684\) Hz before filter margin. Therefore 96 kHz clears that
carrier bound but does not establish transient accuracy or antialiasing.
The resistive harp does not filter these products.

against independently evaluated continuous modal/pickup trajectories, filtered
before decimation. Refine offline reference rates 96→192→384 kHz, continuing
if necessary beyond the runtime API's 192 kHz ceiling. Refine filters separately;

filter latency. Cover high-note attacks/decays, chords and retriggers; report
