negative-zero exception, independently of the codec's floating-point predicate.

and quiet NaNs. Accepted gains preserve their exact bits; signed zeros encode
canonically. Pedal accepts only exact zero/one representations. Valid encodings
and round trips are checked against independently constructed bytes.

at eight alignments, an empty null span, unchanged input storage, and invalid
recall/publication preservation of both pending and published mailbox state.
