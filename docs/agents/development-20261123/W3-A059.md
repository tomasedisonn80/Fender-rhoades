`ASAN_OPTIONS=halt_on_error=1:detect_leaks=0` and
`UBSAN_OPTIONS=halt_on_error=1:print_stacktrace=1` produced **37/37 passes**,
zero skips and empty stderr throughout. No tracing/security settings changed.
Leak detection remains unverified.
