## Public primary sources checked

1. Florian Pfeifle, **Real-time Physical Model of a Wurlitzer and Rhodes Electric Piano**, DAFx 2017, section 5.2 and equation (1): <https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/2017/papers/DAFx17_paper_79.pdf>. The paper uses a compression-dependent elastic term and a compression/velocity-dependent dissipative term for the hammer tip. It motivates viscoelastic contact rather than an instantaneous impulse. Its Rhodes description identifies a neoprene-tipped hammer and felt damper, but neither its parameters nor its simplified mechanical structure identify this repository's future specimen. The paper explicitly simplifies pickup feedback and other physical details.
2. Stefan Bilbao, Alberto Torin and Vasileios Chatziioannou, **Numerical Modeling of Collisions in Musical Instruments**, author preprint: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/1405.2589>; journal record: <https://www.research.ed.ac.uk/en/publications/numerical-modeling-of-collisions-in-musical-instruments/>. Sections 2.1–2.4 develop contact potential, energy-compatible differences, implicit nonlinear solution and nonnegative collision loss. This supports testing the complete energy balance and comparing against a potential-based integrator. The PDF retains draft publication header dates; cite the journal record for the 2015 publication date and DOI 10.3813/AAA.918813.

## Mathematical findings for this repository

The following equations are an independent derivation using the repository's stated coordinates, not copied source code or fitted measurements.

Let `delta = x_h - b^T q - g`, `v_rel = v_h - b^T v`. Apply `-F` to the hammer and `b F` to modal coordinates. Then contact power delivered to kinetic/mechanical states is `-F v_rel`. With elastic contact storage `U = k max(delta,0)^(p+1)/(p+1)`, storage power on the compressed branch is `k delta^p v_rel`. This makes reciprocity and energy bookkeeping directly testable.

For the proposed clipped law `F = max(0, delta^p (k+c v_rel))` at positive compression:

- On the positive-force branch, physical loss is `c delta^p v_rel^2`.
- On the clamped branch, physical loss is `-k delta^p v_rel`, which is nonnegative because clamping requires negative relative velocity when `c>0`.
- At nonpositive compression, force, stored contact energy and this contact loss are zero.

The outer zero-force clamp is an explicit repository model choice. Do not attribute the entire clipped law or the clipped-branch ledger to the cited paper. Geometric compression and force-active contact are separate states. A compressed clamp event is not geometric separation.

For a free two-mass fixture with `b=1`, momentum `m_h v_h + m_r v_r` must remain constant, including with internal contact loss. The relative effective mass is `1/(1/m_h+1/m_r)`. Add a supported modal spring and momentum is no longer an invariant of those two masses alone; its support reaction must be included. Total elastic-plus-kinetic energy remains the appropriate conservative check.

## Recommended next implementation

Implement the specified bounded offline contact reference, with all constants explicitly synthetic. Preserve modal position and velocity on launch/relaunch; record external action work from the exact before/after mechanical and contact energies. State-domain rejection and failure recovery should restore the entire last accepted state and ledger.

Use a potential-based discrete-gradient method as one comparison candidate. For a conservative contact step, the elastic force can use the secant `[U(delta_1)-U(delta_0)]/(delta_1-delta_0)`, with its analytic derivative limit near equal arguments, coupled to midpoint velocity updates. Independently integrate the same continuous equations with refined explicit steps for accuracy comparison. Do not equate a tiny discrete energy residual with waveform or event-time accuracy: a coarse step can still miss a short or grazing collision whose endpoints are both separated.

Initially declare a bounded exponent range with `p>=1`; `0<p<1` introduces a singular onset derivative and needs a separate regularization/validation decision. Freeze SI fixture values and distinct position, velocity, impulse, event-time, force and energy tolerances before results are inspected. Include a genuinely compressed/clamped fixture, a two-free-mass collision, separated analytic motion and a grazing fixture. Restore all accounting state if a step exhausts its iteration/subdivision budget.
