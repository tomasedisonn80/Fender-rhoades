# W1-A016 — Historical action and release limits

**Date:** 2026-11-23. Bounded documentary review; no physical measurements.

**Finding:** The frozen `p = 1` contact reference can establish numerical
correctness within its synthetic equations. It cannot establish Rhodes action,
release, pedal, or loaded-output accuracy. The manufacturer's historical manual
describes mechanisms outside its two-mode, finite-mass-hammer, prescribed-damper
scope ([local acceptance contract](../../specs/CONTACT_ACCEPTANCE_20261123.md)).

## Three omitted mechanisms

1. **Regulated key-to-hammer geometry and differentiated tips.** Chapter 4,
   “Key Dip,” “Escapement,” and “Striking Line,” connects key travel, remaining
   hammer–tine clearance, and strike location; it describes graduated tip height
   and hardness across registers. Changing regulation affects these relationships.
   **Inference:** a velocity-only hammer relaunch with synthetic contact constants
   does not predict the native action's response or register transitions.
   The manual does not identify a force law or establish `p = 1`.
   [Chapter 4](https://www.fenderrhodes.com/org/manual/ch4.html)

2. **Hammer-linked damper motion and leaf-spring restoration.** Chapter 4,
   “Damper Clearance” and “Damper Module Adjustment,” describes a bridle strap
   pulling the damper arm as the hammer rises, maximum clearance at impact,
   and felt bearing against the tine with the hammer resting. Arm tension and
   alignment are adjustable; excessive tension resists hammer motion.
   **Inference:** prescribing a damper face omits that reciprocal linkage and
   does not establish key-release timing or damping accuracy.
   [Chapter 4](https://www.fenderrhodes.com/org/manual/ch4.html)

3. **Common pedal linkage and interactions among released tines.** Chapter 2,
   “Damper Push Rod Assembly,” describes a sustain rod, felt-ended push rod,
   and pivoting release bar disengaging all dampers. Linkage play affects pedal
   sensitivity; the manual explicitly describes sympathetic vibration when all
   tines are released. “Damper Module” also distinguishes arm strength/tension
   and felt geometry across bass, middle, and treble.
   **Inference:** one prescribed damper trajectory cannot establish the real
   pedal-to-clearance mapping, register-dependent release, or sympathetic response.
   [Chapter 2](https://www.fenderrhodes.com/org/manual/ch2.html)

## Evidence boundary and handoff

The [manual index](https://www.fenderrhodes.com/service/manual.html) identifies
the original 1979 manual and places these chapters in its latest-design section;
earlier actions have separate chapters. Applicability to an unidentified Mark I
remains unresolved. No dimensions, hardness readings, or prose descriptions
were converted into masses, stiffnesses, loss constants, or timing measurements.
