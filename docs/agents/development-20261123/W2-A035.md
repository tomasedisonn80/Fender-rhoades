## Delivered

The standard-library CLI reads current frequency, damping, observation and pickup
constants; checks the supported C++ statement forms; and hashes both source files.
It expands the polynomial into **83 complex modal monomials**, retaining signed
mode counts, multinomial multiplicities and observation/polynomial coefficients.
Conjugate/repeated vectors give **31 possible nonzero carrier locations**.

For each key and held/sustained or released regime, it computes damped carrier
frequencies, natural-frequency guard compliance, Nyquist crossings and folded
locations at 32/44.1/48/96/192 kHz. The voltage derivative multiplies each linkage
monomial by its envelope decay minus its signed imaginary angular frequency.
Thus decaying zero-carrier envelopes are retained; stationary undamped DC alone
has zero derivative. Coefficients multiply unspecified complex modal amplitudes,
so entries are not equal-strength lines or guaranteed nonzero after cancellation.
