## Integration and limits

Register the four selectors in the optional SDK build. This is retained synthetic
host-contract evidence, without callback-allocation, performance, non-Linux or
commercial-DAW claims. Inactive buses without supplied sample pointers have no
usable audio silence hint; that hint is deliberately not prescribed. Ownership
is released to the integrator.
