decrease threshold, or 128-ulp roundoff rule changed. Step comparisons hold all
other controls fixed. The two final step differences use the existing budget
and decrease checks; every earlier step difference remains in the output.
Coordinate pairs use matching complete baseline settings at the declared finest
step. Event/local comparisons also use that explicitly declared finest step.

For a nonzero depth, `report.refinement` remains the selected-policy result and
additionally contains:

- `original_frozen_result`: the original-grid result, with its own matrix,
  selected settings/provenance and explicit missing slots.
- `extended_result`: the declared extended-grid result, with the corresponding
  matrix, selected settings/provenance and explicit missing slots.
- `policy`: the declared depth, all axis levels, full baseline local controls,
  output step, original/selected finest steps and contract paths.

All original slots remain mandatory for an extension, even though its new
event/local comparisons use a finer step. Missing retained original axes,
missing finest axes and partial fixture sets remain incomplete. Invalid or
conflicting selected evidence fails. Identifiable finer-run input failures and
missing metadata cannot contaminate the separately reported original result.
Unidentifiable maximum-step metadata is conservatively retained in the original
preflight rather than silently excluded.

remaining a failure beside a passing declared extension. That case uses mocked
comparison values and is a reporting/policy regression, not numerical acceptance.
