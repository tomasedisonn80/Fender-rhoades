## Independent focused cases

The fixture uses orthogonal hammer/damper projections, free modal masses 1 and
0.5 kg, hammer mass 1 kg, zero damping, and initial velocities
`q0'=-0.5`, `q1'=1`, `h'=0.5` m/s. Hammer stiffness is 20000 N/m. Thus the
hammer reduced mass is 0.5 kg and its relative frequency is 200 rad/s.
Damper stiffness sets its independent frequency. Before separation each gap
is `sin(omega*t)/omega`; separation is `pi/omega`, and maximum compression and
force are `1/omega` and `k/omega` at `pi/(2*omega)`. After separation each
contact has analytic free motion, impulse 1 N s, and the total energy remains
0.5 J. The oracle does not call candidate RHS/replay/root routines.

All new cases use a maximum step of 200 microseconds and event-time tolerance
2.5 ns; all other accuracy settings keep their defaults. Both collector modes
must satisfy the same landing and analytic assertions. Peak value bounds are
the existing conservative-peak test's 2e-12 m and 4e-8 N. Event and peak time
assertions use 2.5 ns directly, without widening either event tolerance.

| Selector | Cases and observed result |
| --- | --- |
| `split_simultaneous` | Identical frequencies; both initial onsets and both separations retained, identical separation brackets, deterministic hammer/damper ordering, independent endpoint mechanics/impulses/energy/peaks; 96 checks passed. |
| `split_close_roots` | Damper separation offsets +1 ns, -1 ns, +0.1 ps and -0.1 ps. The 1 ns cases retain disjoint ordered brackets, including damper-first despite hammer-first scanning. The 0.1 ps unequal-frequency cases retain both contact events with overlapping brackets. Both peak modes and continuation pass; 386 checks passed. |
| `split_peak_rollback` | Trial, RHS, event-row and interval-row exhaustion with peaks off and on. Exact typed failures, bounded attempted work, complete public rollback and deterministic continuation; 243 checks passed. |

For every successful pair, accepted mechanics, observations, full ledger,
events and interval histories are bit-identical with peaks enabled/disabled.
Accepted/rejected step counts and every legacy diagnostic, including power
residuals and sampled maxima, are also bit-identical. Only optional records and
charged collector work differ. Another 100 microseconds of continuation keeps
that equality.

The rollback fixture starts from analytic compressed motion 50 ns before
simultaneous separation and accepts a 40 ns warm-up, retaining prior interval
history and nonzero enabled peak records. Its next 20 ns call crosses both
roots and succeeds as two accepted intervals without rejection. Trial/RHS caps
one below that successful call's observed work, and a two-row interval cap,
each fail after one temporary accepted split interval. The one-row event cap
fails during scanning before that commit. Each failure preserves every public
state/configuration/settings/observation/ledger/diagnostic/history field.
Both the rejected object and untouched control then advance 1 ns to identical
complete public results, checking persistent controller/compensated-sum
continuation. Dynamic trial/RHS limits avoid assuming a platform-specific root
iteration count; no production limit is raised.
