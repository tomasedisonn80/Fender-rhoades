## Delivered behavior and provenance

`tools/generate_audio_filter.py` uses only Python's standard library. An original
implementation evaluates Machin's formula for pi and the ordinary arctangent,
sine and Bessel I0 series at fixed 80-digit Decimal precision. It constructs
`N=96L+1`, beta 9, cutoff `0.47 * host_rate`, normalizes DC before rounding once
to binary64, and mirrors one half for bit-exact symmetry. No third-party source
or package was copied/downloaded. The recipe's public window-method reference
is documented in the generator and W1-005.

`include/rhodes/audio_filter_coefficients.hpp` contains exact hexadecimal
`constexpr` literals in `rhodes::audio_filter::coefficients_l1`,
`coefficients_l2`, and `coefficients_l4`, with 97/193/385 taps. Constants expose
`latency_frames=48`, `kaiser_beta=9.0`, and `cutoff_host_rate=0.47`. A compile-time
guard requires IEEE binary64 doubles. There are no timestamps or environment
paths in generated content. The default destination is anchored to the script's
repository, irrespective of the current directory. Writes use whole-file atomic
replacement and refuse symlink destinations. `--check` rejects missing/stale
content without writing files or creating directories.

## Limits and next step

Dense-grid observations are not a continuous-frequency proof. L=1 stopband is
only the Nyquist endpoint. These checks do not establish complete-engine alias
error, audibility, callback cost or physical fidelity. Next: integrate this
header into the fixed-storage FIR and verify phase, ring convolution and the
high-rate core oracle under the separate frozen AudioEngine contract.
